# ONFOOT: Product Requirements Document

## Overview
- Product: ONFOOT, an online futsal booking app
- Platforms: Android and iOS
- Epics: Player Management, Booking Management, Payment and Verification, Futsal Venue Management
- Status: Draft
- Date: 13 November 2026

## Background
Players usually book futsal courts by phone or social media. This causes double booking, unclear availability, and no-shows. ONFOOT confirms a slot only for one verified, prepaid player.

## Objective
A reliable, easy-to-use app where verified players book futsal courts online with prepayment, so each slot belongs to one person.
