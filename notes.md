# My Notes

## What is a PRD?
PRD is the document that says what we are building and who is it for and why. It does not say how to code it.

## What is an epic?
Epic is a big group of related features. ONFOOT has four of them : Booking Management, which holds smaller requirements like View slots and Book a slot.

## What is an assumption?
Assumption is something we believe is true but haven't proved it yet.
